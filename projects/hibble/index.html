Why I made it

I was designing something for a uni project and wanted little hand-drawn annotations on it, the kind of notes you'd scribble in the margin. Every hand-drawn font I could find looked too computerised. None of them looked like real pencil.

I also fancied making a font and wondered how hard it would be, so I made my own. It's called Hibble because it's Harry's scribble: a bit wobbly, a bit uneven, with the pencil grain left in, the way real handwriting is.

1Draw it

Every letter, number and punctuation mark drawn by hand in Procreate, using a pencil brush so the grain stays in.

2Make it a font

Each drawing becomes a character, spaced so the letters sit together like real writing.

3Type with it

A regular weight with capitals, lowercase, numbers and punctuation, from £ to € and curly quotes.
