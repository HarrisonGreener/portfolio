01 The brand

Type the job.
Get a priced quote.

Quotable gives tradespeople their evenings back. Describe a job in your own words and it lists the materials, prices them live at UK merchants and works out the labour, ready to send before you're back in the van.

On the toolsBuilt for people with dusty hands and a phone, not a desk and a spreadsheet.
StraightReal prices, linked. Anything we couldn't price is marked, never guessed.
QuickA quote in about a minute. Everything else gets out of the way.
Trade-madeThe look and the words come from the job: dockets, vans, plasterboard.

03 The tape-measure Q

A Q that measures up.

  1. The case. A heavy navy ring, like the body of a tape measure.
  2. The tape. The tail is the tape pulled out in docket orange, the only colour that means money or action.
  3. The cut. A gap is masked out around the tail, not painted, so the mark sits cleanly on paper, cards, the navy sidebar or a photo.
