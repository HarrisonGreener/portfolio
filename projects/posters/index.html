Graphic design

Twelve
Posters

A poster a day for twelve days, each in a different style. Every morning I pulled the handle on The Great Brandomizer and designed that day’s poster with whatever colours and fonts came out.

Posters12, one a day
Time30 minutes each
FormatA2 portrait
AssetsThe Great Brandomizer
RoleDesign

The rule

One day, one spin, one poster, and thirty minutes on the clock for the research and the design together. Each poster sits with the brand card it was made from, and the colours and fonts on that card are the only ones allowed.
