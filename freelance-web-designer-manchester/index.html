A local designer who builds

I'm based in Manchester, where I'm studying Digital Media and Communications at Manchester Metropolitan University, and I've been freelancing as a web designer since 2024. Most designers hand a design over to someone else to build. I do both, so the site you approve is the site that goes live.

I work with trades, shops, cafés, startups and agencies in and around the city: anyone who needs a website that looks the part and is easy to find on Google, or a brand that holds together from the shop sign to the Instagram grid. I also run my own limited company, Quotable, so I know what a small business needs from its website: it has to bring in work.

Yes. I do branding, logos, web design and web development, so you can get your brand and website from one person.

Every site I build is fast, works on phones, and is set up with the basics Google looks for, such as page titles, descriptions, structured data and a sitemap.
